Ziwei Ma

Associate Professor of Statistics, University of Tennessee at Chattanooga

Google ScholarResearchGateE-mail

I am an associate professor of statistics in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Tennessee at Chattanooga (UTC), where I have been on the faculty since August 2020. I also work part time as a biostatistician at the University of Tennessee College of Medicine - Chattanooga.

Previously, I earned my Ph.D. in Mathematics (concentration in Mathematical Statistics) from New Mexico State University in Las Cruces, NM, where I was fortunate to be advised by Professor Tonghui Wang.

E-mail: ziwei-ma@utc.edu

Research interests

  • Statistics: linear models, skew-normal distributions, inferential models, clinical trials
  • Applied mathematics and statistics: machine learning in engineering, applications of stochastic differential equations in biology and medical science

Education

  • Ph.D. in Mathematics (Concentration: Mathematical Statistics), New Mexico State University, 2020
  • M.S. in Mathematics, New Mexico State University, 2015
  • M.S. in Mathematics, Northwest University, 2007
  • B.S. in Mathematics, Northwest University, 2004

Professional experience

  • August 2026 - present: Associate Professor at UTC
  • August 2021 - present: Biostatistician (part time) at the University of Tennessee College of Medicine - Chattanooga
  • August 2020 - July 2026: Assistant Professor at UTC
  • January 2014 - May 2020: Teaching/Research Assistant at New Mexico State University
