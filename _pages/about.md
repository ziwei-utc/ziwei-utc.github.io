I am an associate professor of statistics in the [Department of Mathematics](https://new.utc.edu/directory/djd955-mathematics-ziwei-ma/djd955) at the [University of Tennessee at Chattanooga (UTC)](https://www.utc.edu/), where I have been on the faculty since August 2020. I also work part time as a biostatistician at the University of Tennessee College of Medicine - Chattanooga.

Previously, I earned my Ph.D. in Mathematics (concentration in Mathematical Statistics) from [New Mexico State University](https://www.nmsu.edu/) in [Las Cruces](https://www.las-cruces.org/), NM, where I was fortunate to be advised by Professor [Tonghui Wang](https://math.nmsu.edu/people/facultydirectory/tonghui-tony-wang.html).

E-mail: [ziwei-ma@utc.edu](mailto:ziwei-ma@utc.edu)

Research interests
======
* **Statistics:** linear models, skew-normal distributions, inferential models, clinical trials
* **Applied mathematics and statistics:** machine learning in engineering, applications of stochastic differential equations in biology and medical science
